# What job applications ask

Research run on 29 Sep 2026 to decide which questions Job Hunter's saved answers should cover. Nothing was submitted: Greenhouse and Lever forms were read from their public posting data, Ashby forms were opened and read in a browser, and a Workday application was followed only as far as its sign-in step.

**Sample:** 145 live applications across 22 companies (108 Greenhouse, 30 Lever, 7 Ashby). Mostly tech employers, because those boards are public; Workday employers (retail, healthcare, banking) couldn't be sampled past sign-in.

## Other findings

- **Workday requires an account first.** Step 1 of 6 is "Create Account/Sign In", before any questions (then My Information, My Experience, Application Questions, Voluntary Disclosures, Review). The helper never creates accounts or enters passwords, so it pauses at this step (“Sign in needed”) until the person signs in themselves, then carries on.
- **Location fields autocomplete.** 71 Greenhouse forms had hidden latitude/longitude fields behind a city picker, so the city has to be chosen from the suggestions, not just typed.
- **Company-specific questions are common but rarely repeat** (e.g. "Are you currently a Wealthfront client?"). "Your own questions" plus "Save this answer" covers these.
- Salary and start date were rarer than expected in this sample (4% and 2%).
